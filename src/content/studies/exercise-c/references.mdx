## References

1. J. A. Brzozowski. **On aperiodic I-monoids.** Technical Report CS-75-28, Department of Computer Science, University of Waterloo, November 1975.

2. Dominique Perrin and Jean-Éric Pin. **Semigroups and automata on infinite words.** In _Semigroups, Formal Languages and Groups_, pages 49-72. Kluwer Academic Publishers, 1995. Proceedings of the NATO Advanced Study Institute, York, 1993.

3. Thomas Wilke. **An Eilenberg theorem for ω-languages.** In _Automata, Languages and Programming_, pages 588-599. Springer, Berlin, Heidelberg, 1991.

The characterization of definite languages by right-zero idempotents is attributed to Brzozowski. The linked-pair construction follows Perrin and Pin, and the two-sorted algebraic framework follows Wilke.

## Terminology note

The phrase **ω-trivial** is used in this series for the condition $|S_\omega|=1$. This is a statement about the infinite sort constructed from the finite semigroup. When the unique class is chosen as accepting, the recognized ω-language is $\Sigma^\omega$; when no class is chosen, it is $\varnothing$.
